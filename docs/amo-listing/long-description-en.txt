**Macedonian spelling and grammar checking, entirely on your device.** No text ever leaves your machine — no server, no internet, no analytics. It works offline, inside your browser.

Macedonian is not supported by LanguageTool, Grammarly, or any other serious proofreading tool. Lektor-MK is an attempt at the first real one: spelling and grammar that run completely on your own computer.

## What it checks

13 rules, each with positive and negative tests:

- **Spelling** — 427,464 forms in a compact FST (1.25 MB), with suggestions ranked by frequency.
- **Mixed scripts** — Latin letters hidden inside Cyrillic words (`мaкeдoнски`), invisible to the eye.
- **Foreign Cyrillic letters** — Serbian `ђ ћ`, Russian `ъ ы э я ю`, Bulgarian `щ`.
- **Latin-transliterated Macedonian** — converted back to Cyrillic.
- **Double definiteness** — `убавата книгата` → `убавата книга`.
- **Clitic order** — `ми го даде` ✓, `го ми даде` ✗.
- **Dative ѝ** — `ѝ` as opposed to the conjunction `и`.
- **L-participle agreement** — `таа дошол` ✗, does not agree with its subject.
- **Fused не** — `несака` → `не сака`.
- **Separated нај and по** — `нај добар` → `најдобар`, `по добар` → `подобар`.
- **Punctuation** — lowercase sentence start, space before a closing comma or period.

## How you use it

As you type in any text field, flagged words get a wavy underline. Click an underline to get a card with the rule name, a short explanation, and a suggested fix you can apply with one click. If the word is correct, add it to your personal dictionary.

The toolbar popup offers a quick check of pasted text and next-word autocomplete (from a bigram table). Settings give you a master switch, a check delay, a personal dictionary, per-site pausing, and a toggle for every individual rule.

## Privacy

- Nothing is collected, stored, transmitted, or shared.
- Zero network requests. No analytics, no telemetry, no accounts.
- `storage` — only your settings and personal dictionary, kept on your device.
- `activeTab` — to recognise the current site for per-site pausing.
- `clipboardWrite` — to copy a suggestion when you click it.

## Accuracy

Every rule is precision-gated: it does not ship unless it reports zero false positives on a Macedonian Wikipedia sample. The flag rate is 2.78% over 17,104 words, and `MK_DOUBLE_DEFINITE` has 0 false positives across 17,782 words of edited prose. Rules change; the bar does not: precision before recall.

## Compatibility

- Firefox 140+ on desktop, and Firefox for Android 142+.
- About 2.2 MB to download, about 6.2 MB installed.

## License and source

GPL-3.0-or-later. Everything is open: the base wordlist (gerazov/dictionary-mk, GPL-2.0), the paradigms expanded from Apertium (GPL), and counts only from Macedonian Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0) — Wikipedia article text is never distributed.

The engine is Rust compiled to WebAssembly, with no I/O and no platform assumptions. The same code backs the test CLI and a future desktop build.
